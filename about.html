OUR STORY

Rooted in people.
Inspired by place.

My Own Decor is a new design studio built around a simple idea: a beautiful space should feel effortless to live in. We combine practical planning with a calm, contemporary aesthetic, taking time to understand the people who will call each space home.

From the first conversation to the finishing touches, our process is collaborative, transparent and detail-focused. We want every decision to have a reason — and every room to feel right.
